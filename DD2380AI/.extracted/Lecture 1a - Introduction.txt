Gartner’s Hype Cycle
• A model that describes the typical progression of new
  technologies from initial excitement to mainstream
  adoption. The hype cycle has five stages:
1. Innovation Trigger – A new technology emerges and
   garners interest.
2. Peak of Inflated Expectations – Hype builds as people
   start seeing potential applications, often leading to
   overblown expectations.
3. Trough of Disillusionment – The initial excitement fades,
   and limitations or challenges become more apparent.
4. Slope of Enlightenment – Some companies and users
   continue developing and understanding the technology's
   practical uses and realistic applications.
5. Plateau of Productivity – The technology matures,
   stabilizes, and finds its niche in the market, leading to
   mainstream adoption and steady progress.

Environment properties

Property                        Meaning
Fully vs partially observable   Does the agent have access to all relevant state information?
Deterministic vs stochastic     Does an action uniquely determine the next state?
Episodic vs sequential          Do previous actions affect future decisions?
Static vs dynamic               Can the environment change while the agent is deciding?
Discrete vs continuous          Are states/actions/time discrete or continuous?
Single vs multi-agent           Are there other agents whose actions matter?
Cooperative vs competitive      Are the agents' objectives aligned or opposed?
